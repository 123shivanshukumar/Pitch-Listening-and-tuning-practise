# Pitch-Listening-and-tuning-practise

An app to sharpen your ear to specific pitches. Set a pitch name (like C#3 or B5) or just enter the freqeuncy (in Hz, like 440), click on `Set target`. This sets the target you want to achieve by adjusting the pitch of a tunable meter. 

To listen to the target pitch you can either play repeated plucks, or a continuous drone, by selecting from the `Sound` drop down, and clicking on `Play target`. You can also set a random frequency by clicking on the `Random` button. 

You can adjust the pitch of the meter by coarse and fine tuning, by moving along the respective sliders. 

You can toggle to see the current and target pitches individually. 

Finally, check how far your adjusted pitch is from the target by clicking on the `Check` button. 

# Running
Just open the html file in any browser.
